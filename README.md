# Electric Phi

Electric Phi is an electric transportation, energy, materials and infrastructure research/commerce channel for the Infinity network.

## Scope

The project is intended to organize information about electric cars, autonomous-driving systems, trains, aircraft, drones, boats, batteries, charging systems, energy equipment, construction materials and international suppliers. Chinese EVs and other imports are important research categories, alongside products from other markets.

## Experience

Electric Phi should combine:

- searchable research and media cards
- vehicle/product specifications and comparisons
- manufacturer and supplier sources
- pricing and availability observations with timestamps
- infrastructure and charging information
- related News Phi stories
- Shop Phi paths when a researched item is actually purchasable
- Quant connections between technologies, places, manufacturers, materials and searches

## Accuracy rules

Safety, range, autonomy/FSD capability, regulatory approval, import eligibility, tariffs/taxes, pricing and availability can change by jurisdiction and date. Electric Phi should source and timestamp those claims rather than hard-code statements such as “no tax imports” or assume that a vehicle legal in one market can be imported or operated in another.

Comparisons should expose measurable attributes—range, charging, dimensions, price, safety data and feature availability—rather than declaring a manufacturer universally better.

## Planned flow

`topic -> research -> specifications/media -> related Quants -> current news -> comparison -> optional shopping/import path`

## Integration

Electric Phi is designed to use Quants for relationship expansion, News Phi for fresh reporting, Shop Phi for commerce, and Monitor Phi for deployment/runtime infrastructure.

## Status

Architecture repository. Planned capabilities become production claims only after they are implemented and verified.
